Project Charter

CampusCents is a proposed personal finance management system designed to help college students organize their spending, budgets, bills, and savings goals.

Download the Project Charter PDF

Project Purpose

Many college students have difficulty tracking how much money they spend throughout the week or month. Small purchases can add up quickly and make it harder to follow a budget.

CampusCents is intended to help users organize their financial information and better understand their spending habits. This project will focus on planning and documentation rather than developing a working software prototype.

Project Objectives

  • Identify the financial needs of college students.
  • Define the proposed features of CampusCents.
  • Create clear project-management documentation.
  • Identify the project's stakeholders and risks.
  • Develop a realistic project schedule and estimate.
  • Present the project through the Living Project Portal.

Project Scope

In Scope

  • Market research
  • Business strategy
  • Project charter
  • Project requirements and backlog
  • Stakeholder and risk analysis
  • Project schedule and estimates
  • Sprint documents and change logs
  • Living Project Portal
  • Final presentation

Out of Scope

  • Development of a working software prototype
  • Direct connections to bank accounts
  • Payment processing
  • Collection of real financial information
  • Investment or tax advice
  • Credit scoring
  • Loans or lending services

Proposed Features

  • Income and expense tracking
  • Monthly budget categories
  • Spending summaries
  • Savings goals
  • Bill reminders
  • Spending alerts
  • Basic financial reports

Major Deliverables

  • Living Project Portal
  • Market Research document
  • Business Strategy document
  • Project Charter
  • Project requirements and backlog
  • Project schedule and estimates
  • Risk and stakeholder documentation
  • Sprint retrospectives and change logs
  • Final project presentation

Assumptions and Constraints

The project will focus on documentation, and a working prototype is not required. The team will use free tools to create and host the website. No real banking information will be collected.

The project must be completed within the semester. Team members have different schedules, and all documents must follow the course requirements and deadlines.

Stakeholders

  • College students: The intended users of the proposed system.
  • Instructor: Reviews the project and provides feedback.
  • Project team: Researches, plans, and documents the project.
  • University community: May benefit from improved student financial awareness.

Key Risks

  • Adding too many features could increase the project scope.
  • Incomplete research could weaken project decisions.
  • Documents may contain inconsistent information.
  • Unclear responsibilities could cause missed deadlines.
  • Broken links could make portal documents unavailable.

Success Criteria

  • All required documents are completed.
  • The documents are clear and consistent.
  • The proposed features and scope are clearly explained.
  • Each public document is available as webpage text and PDF.
  • The Living Project Portal is organized and easy to use.
  • All Sprint 1 requirements are submitted on time.
  • Every team member can explain the project decisions.
