Project Charter

CampusCents is a proposed personal finance management system designed to help college students organize their income, expenses, budgets, bills, and savings goals.

This project focuses on creating complete project-management documentation for the proposed system. A working software prototype is not included in the project scope.

Download the Project Charter PDF

Project Purpose

Many college students have difficulty understanding where their money goes and how much they can safely spend each month. CampusCents proposes a simple finance-management system that would allow students to record transactions, create budgets, monitor spending, receive bill reminders, and track savings progress.

The purpose of this project is to develop the planning, research, strategy, scope, requirements, estimates, risks, and other project-management documents needed to describe how CampusCents could be developed successfully.

Project Objectives

  • Identify the financial-management needs of college students.
  • Define the proposed functions and requirements of CampusCents.
  • Establish a clear project scope and prevent unnecessary scope growth.
  • Identify the project's stakeholders, assumptions, constraints, and risks.
  • Develop a realistic project schedule and cost estimate.
  • Maintain complete and consistent project-management documentation.
  • Present the project information through a professional Living Project Portal.

Proposed System Features

The project documentation will describe the following proposed CampusCents features:

  • User profile management
  • Manual income and expense entry
  • Customizable spending categories
  • Monthly budget planning
  • Budget and spending summaries
  • Savings-goal tracking
  • Bill reminders
  • Basic financial reports

Out of Scope

  • Development of a working software prototype
  • Programming or implementing the proposed features
  • Direct connections to real bank accounts
  • Payment processing
  • Collection of real personal financial information
  • Investment recommendations
  • Tax preparation or professional financial advice
  • Credit-score calculations
  • Cryptocurrency trading
  • Loans or lending services

Assumptions

  • The project will focus on project-management documentation.
  • A working finance-management prototype is not required.
  • The team will use free tools to create and host the portal.
  • The project will use research and hypothetical financial examples.
  • No real banking or personal financial information will be collected.
  • Team members will complete and review their assigned work before submission.
  • The project requirements may be revised after receiving instructor or peer feedback.
  • Course deadlines will determine the project schedule.

Constraints

  • The project must be completed within the academic semester.
  • The team has no budget for paid software or hosting services.
  • Team members have different schedules and availability.
  • All required documents must follow the course guidelines.
  • Every public document must appear as website text and as a downloadable PDF.
  • Private documents, including sprint retrospectives and peer evaluations, cannot be published on the website.

Key Project Risks

  • Scope growth: Adding unnecessary features or documents could delay the project.
  • Incomplete research: Limited market information could weaken the team's project decisions.
  • Inconsistent documentation: Information may conflict across different project documents.
  • Unclear responsibilities: Poor task ownership could result in incomplete or late work.
  • Communication problems: Missed messages or meetings could affect team coordination.
  • Technical website problems: Broken links, incorrect file paths, or hosting problems could make portal content unavailable.
  • Missed deadlines: Delayed reviews or submissions could reduce the quality of the final work.

Success Criteria

  • All required project-management documents are completed.
  • The documents are clear, accurate, and consistent with one another.
  • The proposed system's scope and features are clearly defined.
  • Every public document is available as website text and as a downloadable PDF.
  • The Living Project Portal is organized, professional, and easy to navigate.
  • Private documents are submitted only through Blackboard.
  • All Sprint requirements are submitted by their deadlines.
  • Every team member can explain the project's documents and decisions.
