Candidate Risk Entries

The following risks were identified based on competitor research and the interviews conducted. These represent potential challenges that CampusCents may face and propose strategies to address them.

CR-01 Slow Access to Financial Information

  • Risks: Users may not use CampusCents if checking their financial spending requires too many steps.
  • Trigger: If checking available balance is an inconvenient or time consuming process.
  • Proposed Owner: UI/UX Lead.
  • Mitigation: Provide a useful dashboard that displays available funds immediately without needing for additional interaction.
  • Evidence ID: LOG-01 (Cleo).

CR-02 Insufficient Competitive Advantage

  • Risks: Users may prefer established applications if CampusCents does not offer useful features.
  • Trigger: Users cannot recognize meaningful advantages over current budgeting applications.
  • Proposed Owner: Product Lead.
  • Mitigation: Utilize AI to identify overspending and suggest adjustments to help users stay within their planned budgets.
  • Evidence ID: LOG-02 (Monarch Money).

CR-03 Excessive Manual Maintenance

  • Risks: Users may stop using CampusCents if the process of managing transactions and budget categories requires too much manual effort.
  • Trigger: Users can experience frustration with the system if corrections are needed frequently or budget updates require too much manual input.
  • Proposed Owner: Development Lead.
  • Mitigation: Implement an automated transaction categorization and a simplified review for transactions requiring the user’s attention.
  • Evidence ID: LOG-03 (YNAB).
