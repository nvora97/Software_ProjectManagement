Sprint 2

In Sprint 2, we took the business strategy and evolved it into a more defined project overview, business value statement and GO recommendation. Our main goal is still to help people get a better understanding of their finances with less manual work. The new language is more focused on the application’s functions, convenience and support for financial objectives.

What Changed and Why

Document Structure

  • What changed: Business Objective, Strategy-to-Project Chain, and Strategic Justification became Project Overview, Business Value, and Recommendation.
  • Reason or effect: Makes the proposed application, its value, and the decision to proceed easier to identify.

Audience Wording

  • What changed: Sprint 1 specifically referred to students. Sprint 2 uses the broader term “users.”
  • Reason or effect: Makes the description more general. The text does not establish whether this represents an intentional expansion beyond students.

Project Focus

  • What changed: Sprint 2 emphasizes recording and categorizing expenses, managing budgets, and monitoring progress toward financial goals.
  • Reason or effect: Explains more directly what the application is intended to help users do.

Business Value

  • What changed: Sprint 2 more explicitly compares the convenience of one system with maintaining spreadsheets or notes.
  • Reason or effect: Shows the practical benefit of organizing financial activity in one accessible place.

Guidance and Savings Goals

  • What changed: Sprint 2 explicitly includes spending recommendations and progress toward personal saving goals.
  • Reason or effect: Connects financial organization to ongoing budget management and longer-term goals.

Research Detail

  • What changed: Sprint 2 condenses the interview examples and no longer explicitly describes safe-to-spend visibility or early overspending warnings.
  • Reason or effect: Keeps the overview shorter, but makes the connection between research findings and proposed features less visible.

Recommendation

  • What changed: Sprint 2 adds an explicit GO recommendation.
  • Reason or effect: Records the recommendation to proceed. The stated support is the expected value of organization and convenience; no cost or feasibility analysis appears in these sections.

Cumulative Effect

The Sprint 2 version more directly explains the application and its expected benefits. It maintains the focus on simpler financial management but clarifies the savings goal and the recommendation to proceed. A short link to the Sprint 1 interview findings would also help explain why the selected functions are important to students.
