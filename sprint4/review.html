CampusCents Sprint 4 Review

Service Levels | Blameless Incident Review

This review addresses the unclear audience wording carried over from our Sprint 2 business case and flagged in Sprint 3. We need a clearer review process to ensure consistency in project scope and supporting research.

Summary

Our strategy for Sprint 1 was for students, but the business case for Sprint 2 used the more general term “users.” We called this out in our Sprint 3 change note, noting that the docs did not determine if the broader audience was intentional. This was an issue of documentation and scope clarification.

Impact

The wording made it less clear to readers who our target audience was and made it harder to link the business case back to the original student research. It could also lead to different interpretations of which needs should drive decisions about features. Available records do not show any downtime, data loss or a measured delay in delivery.

Timeline

  • Sprint 1: The strategy identified the target audience as students.
  • Sprint 2: The business case referred to users, encouraging an organizational and convenient approach.
  • Sprint 3: The change note recorded the different wording; my contradiction memo recommended retaining student focus, linking features and research findings.
  • Sprint 4: This review proposes follow-up checks. Completion dates awaited team confirmation.

Contributing Causes

The gap documented was the audience wording changed but there wasn't a clear scope decision alongside it. The shorter business case also reduced visibility of the link to interview findings. A likely process gap to confirm with the team is that there isn't an explicit audience and evidence check before publication.

What Went Well

The way we see it, Sprint 3 comparison made it visible. The contradiction memo used Cleo, Monarch Money, and YNAB findings to explain why convenience alone might not be sufficient. These records gave us concrete improvements to point at, without blaming a team member.
