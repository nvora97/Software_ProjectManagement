Escalation

Download Escalation PDF

Our escalation process showcases how the CampusCents team responds to service failures, including the communication of these incidents and how responsibilities will be assigned.

1. Severity Levels and Response Times

Severity Trigger Start Fixing Notify Instructor
Severity 1 - Critical Incorrect financial calculations, failed transaction synchronization, or portal outage within 48 hours of a deadline. Within 2 hours; initiate automatic rollback for financial discrepancies. Within 4 hours
Severity 2 - Major A required portal page is broken. Within 24 hours Weekly update
Severity 3 - Minor Outdated documentation or minor issues that do not affect functionality. By the next weekly review Only if repeated

2. Incident Roles and Responsibilities

  • Incident Commander: Nairuti Vora
    • Evaluates the severity of the situation, assigns responsibilities, and coordinates the response.
  • Fixers: Hector Pariente and Francisco Vazquez
    • Frontend/AI Integration Lead and Backend/QA Lead.
    • Responsible for investigating and resolving technical issues.
  • Communicator: Kinley Tenzin
    • Updates the team and instructor on incident status and resolution.

3. Decision Rights

The Incident Commander determines the priorities for the incident at hand and can suspend deployments or authorize manual rollbacks. Financial discrepancies trigger the automatic rollback policy established in Target 2. Only designated fixers are authorized to modify the system during an incident.

4. Communication Procedure

All incidents will be documented in the team's issue log. Severity 1 incidents require immediate notification, instructor notification within 4 hours, and team status updates every 2 hours until resolved.

Severity 2 incidents will be included in the weekly instructor update. Severity 3 incidents will be reviewed during regular team meetings and escalated if they become recurring problems.
