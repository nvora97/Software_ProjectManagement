Change Log

Change ID Sprint Scope Change Reason for Change Impact
CH-01 Sprint 1 Added AI-powered personalized spending recommendations to help users reduce unnecessary spending and increase savings. Interview feedback specifically requested recommendations for cutting expenses and understanding spending breakdowns. Small increase in AI functionality and testing.
CH-02 Sprint 1 Added AI financial Q&A so users can ask questions about their finances and receive understandable, personalized answers. Interviews identified a need for automated insights, including understanding safe spending limits and receiving financial guidance. Small increase in AI development and testing.
CH-03 Sprint 1 Expanded spending alerts to include warnings when spending is unusually high or users are approaching/exceeding their budget. Interview feedback showed users wanted warnings before they went over budget or spent too quickly. Minor increase in alert/notification functionality.
CH-04 Sprint 1 Refined spending tracking to include automatic categorization rather than relying only on manual expense entry. Interviews indicated that manual entry was considered tedious and users wanted automatic categorization. Minor increase in data-processing and integration work.
