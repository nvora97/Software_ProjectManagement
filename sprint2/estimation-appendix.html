Scope Estimated

We estimated the AI chatbot and personalized financial recommendations. These features help users understand their financial situation, reduce unnecessary spending, and increase savings. This estimate covers only these AI features, not the entire application.

Methods Used

Use-Case Points

We counted one user actor and two use cases: financial questions and personalized recommendations. This resulted in 23 use-case points. Using an assumed rate of 20 hours per point, the estimate is 460 team hours. Technical and environment factors were each assumed to be 1.0.

Analogous Estimation

We used Copilot Money as a comparable product. The estimate assumes a two-person team and 18 months of development. We assumed our AI features represent 10–15% of that effort and used 160 hours per person-month. This produced a range of 576–864 team hours. No numerical team-size adjustment was applied.

Estimated Range

Estimates for the AI chatbot and recommendation features

Expected duration About 5.5 weeks

The expected effort uses a most likely value of 650 hours, combined with the low and high estimates using PERT. The duration assumes four people each working 30 productive hours per week.

Reasoning and Assumptions

The methods differ because one counts our user goals while the other uses a similar product as a reference. The most likely estimate of 650 hours allows for potential additional complexity in AI integration, testing, and personalization.

All productivity figures and rates are planning assumptions. Copilot’s assumed 18-month development duration has not been verified. External AI service actors and integration effort still need confirmation. Separate hosting and API usage costs are not included in the cost range.
