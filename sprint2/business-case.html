Business Case

Project Overview

The purpose of our proposed Finance Management Application is to help users better organize and understand their personal finances. The scope of this project is based on providing a centralized approach to better keep track of expenses, manage budgets and achieve financial goals.

The application looks to bring the most important financial management aspects into a single system that can prove simple and accessible in its use. The main functions will focus on keeping a record on expenses and providing categories for them, while also allowing users to monitor their progress.

Business Value

The main value of this project lies in providing the users with a simplified way to manage financial information. In today’s world, keeping track of every transaction can be a juggling exercise that can easily become an overwhelming task. Through the use of this application users can review their financial activity in one place, through a user-friendly interface instead of relying on manually keeping updates on personal spreadsheets or notes.

The application can provide assistance on spending habits and can give recommendations on how to maintain budgets in a consistent manner and be able to continuously progress in reaching persona saving goals. Therefore, the application provides value through organization and convenience.
