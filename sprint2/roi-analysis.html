Investment Overview

CampusCents is a personal finance management application that provides users with a centralized way to track expenses, manage budgets, monitor financial activity, and make progress toward financial goals. The main business value of CampusCents comes from making personal financial management simpler and more convenient by bringing important financial information together in one place. This can help users better understand their spending habits, consistently manage their budgets, and make more informed financial decisions.

Project Investment

For Sprint 2, the team estimated the AI Financial Recommendations portion of CampusCents, which consists of a financial assistant chatbot with personalized recommendations and spending insights. As part of the team's Analogous Estimation method, the AI slice was assumed to represent approximately 10–15% of the comparable product's first release, providing a rough indication of the relative size of this functionality rather than an exact percentage of the full CampusCents project. The team used Analogous Estimation and Use-Case Points to estimate this portion. The final effort range was 460 to 864 team hours, with an expected effort of approximately 654 hours. Using the assumed loaded labor rate of $60 per hour, the estimated cost ranges from $27,600 to $51,840, with an expected development investment of approximately $39,240. With a four-person team working an assumed 30 productive hours per person each week, development is estimated to take approximately 5.5 weeks.

ROI and Conclusion

The expected return from this investment comes from the value CampusCents can provide to its users. The initial return on investment can be evaluated through outcomes such as user adoption, continued usage, and reductions in unnecessary spending. Once CampusCents is deployed, actual results can be compared with development costs to calculate a more accurate financial ROI. Based on the expected benefits and current estimates, this analysis supports the Business Case's recommendation to GO with the project.
